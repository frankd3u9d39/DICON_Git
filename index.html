Terms & Privacy

1. GITH Platform Mandate

The Grassroot Innovations Talent Hunt (GITH) is initiated under DICON to discover, evaluate, and nurture Nigerian innovators, developers, makers, and technology entrepreneurs.

2. Intellectual Property (IP)

Before submitting confidential technical information, applicants should consider appropriate intellectual-property protection. GITH submission does not itself constitute patent, copyright, trademark or other IP registration. Applicants retain full ownership of their intellectual property.

3. Data Privacy & Handling

All personal and technical records are stored securely within Firestore and Firebase Cloud infrastructure under Nigerian data protection compliance. Evaluators access submitted proposals under strict non-disclosure obligations.
