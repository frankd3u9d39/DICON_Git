Institutional Charter & Operational Framework

Section 01

Introduction to GITH

Grassroot Innovations Talent Hunt (GITH) is an innovation discovery and talent development initiative designed to identify, document, assess, nurture, and promote talented innovators, inventors, problem-solvers, developers, researchers, students, artisans, makers, and technology enthusiasts across Nigeria.

GITH is founded on the recognition that valuable ideas, technical skills, inventive abilities, and practical solutions exist in communities throughout the country. However, many innovators lack access to institutional support, technical expertise, development facilities, mentorship, and networks required to advance their ideas. GITH bridges this gap by establishing a structured pathway through which grassroots innovators are systematically discovered, documented, and connected to national development pipelines.

Section 02

DICON’s Institutional Role & Mandate

DICON provides the institutional anchor for GITH, subject to its approved mandate and governance arrangements. GITH serves as a grassroots discovery mechanism, while DICON provides the institutional context for technical assessment, refinement, and engagement with advanced facilities.

A

Institutional Direction & Coordination

Providing strategic governance, institutional alignment, and establishing high standards for talent development matching national technological priorities.

B

Technical Assessment & Evaluation

Facilitating access to defense and engineering experts to evaluate functionality, feasibility, build quality, and real-world applicability.

C

Innovation Development Pathways

Establishing structured milestones through which promising concepts progress through prototyping, testing, technical refinement, and research collaboration.

D

Technical Mentorship & Capacity

Pairing innovators with seasoned engineers, military technologists, and industry specialists to upgrade skills and build production-grade prototypes.

E

Infrastructure & Institutional Linkages

Opening authorized access to state-of-the-art workshops, precision laboratories, defense fabrication units, and research facilities.

F

Technology Transfer & Application

Supporting pathways for pilot deployment, industrial adoption, intellectual property safeguarding, and potential commercialization.

G

Innovation Records & Institutional Learning

Maintaining an authenticated nationwide registry of grassroots capabilities to inform policy, research planning, and national self-reliance.

Section 03

The Relationship Between GITH & DICON

The synergy operates through a clear division of complementary functions designed to prevent talented inventors from being overlooked.

Discovery Arm

GITH Discovers Talent

Reaches into communities, technical colleges, makerspaces, and rural clusters across all 36 States and the FCT to identify innovators, document inventions, and register technical capabilities.

Institutional Anchor

DICON Directs & Refines

Within its defense and industrial mandate, DICON provides technical scrutiny, access to engineering facilities, mentorship, and high-level evaluation for national security and economic impact.

Ecosystem Enablers

Wider Innovation Network

Universities, research institutes, industrial manufacturers, patent agencies, technology hubs, and investors provide specialized resources to scale viable solutions nationwide.

Section 04

Core Objectives & Capabilities

GITH is structured to perform 12 mission-critical activities for Nigerian inventors:

01. Discover Hidden Talent

Uncovering inventive minds outside major metropolitan tech centers.

02. Document Prototypes

Capturing schematics, bill of materials, working mechanisms, and test logs.

03. Structured National Database

Maintaining a verified national inventory of indigenous innovations.

04. Grassroots Participation

Empowering technical colleges, roadside fabricators, and artisans.

05. Presentation Platform

Giving inventors an official, high-visibility platform to showcase solutions.

06. Rigorous Verification

Performing preliminary screening, integrity checks, and peer reviews.

07. Technical Investigation

Selecting high-potential designs for lab validation and stress-testing.

08. Development Linkages

Connecting eligible builders with institutional grants and equipment.

09. Incubation & Prototyping

Guiding crude prototypes into refined, commercially viable hardware.

10. Institutional Collaboration

Forging ties between defense researchers and local maker communities.

11. National Spotlights

Demonstrating Nigerian ingenuity at defense exhibitions and expos.

12. Tech Commercialization

Paving pathways toward patenting, mass manufacturing, and adoption.

Section 05

Who Can Participate?

GITH welcomes innovators from all technical backgrounds and walks of life. An innovation does NOT have to be a finished commercial product to qualify!

Sections 07 – 09

Vision, Mission & Guiding Philosophy

Our Vision

A National Discovery Network

To establish a nationwide grassroots innovation discovery network that connects Nigeria’s untapped talent with institutional expertise and opportunities to transform promising ideas into practical solutions.

Our Mission

Structured Pathways to Impact

To systematically discover, document, assess, and nurture grassroots innovators across Nigeria while creating structured pathways through which promising innovations access technical guidance, institutional support, mentorship, and wider networks.

Guiding Philosophy

Innovation Knows No Boundary

Innovation should be discovered wherever it exists. An innovative solution may emerge from a rural workshop, school lab, or roadside artisan. Its origin should never preclude serious institutional evaluation.

Sections 10 & 11

From Grassroots to Institutional Reality

Building Nigeria's self-reliance by transforming crude grassroots inventions into robust industrial assets.

By combining grassroots discovery with institutional coordination and appropriate technical development pathways, GITH contributes directly to a more connected, sovereign, and self-sufficient Nigerian defense and industrial ecosystem.

Our Commitment: GITH promotes an innovation discovery process that is transparent, accessible, intellectually safeguarded, and focused on practical, high-impact problem solving for the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
